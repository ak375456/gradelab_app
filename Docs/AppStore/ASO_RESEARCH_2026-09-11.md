## Description and monetization verification
Checked ProCatalog.swift, LUT selection/import code, export gates, scope access, saved presets and typography.
- Free: basic adjustments; Master/RGB curves; HSL; wheels; fade/sharpen/vignette; histogram; overlay layers and blend modes; supported keyframe animation; ten looks (Warm Cinema, Teal Orange, Soft Film, Cinematic, Vintage Fox, Portrait 160, Moody Aqua, Palm Springs, Bold Film, Serenity); supported SDR exports up to 1080p and full-resolution JPEG when the edit uses free features.
- Pro: premium/custom LUT exports, advanced color curves, grain/bloom/glow/halation, saved grading presets, measurement scopes, supported HDR/Apple Log workflows, ProRes, higher video resolutions, export controls, HEIC/PNG, and imported/premium fonts.

- Imported LUTs/fonts can currently be brought into the editor for preview; the implementation gates their exported use.
- No claims of AI editing, RAW support, universal Log support, batch editing, LUT-file generation or exact film-stock replication are made.
