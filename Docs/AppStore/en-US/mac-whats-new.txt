GradeLab for Mac.

The same color grading editor, the same projects and the same LUTs — laid out for a desktop window.

A MEDIA BIN
Every clip, photo and audio file in your project in a column of its own, with a thumbnail, its length or size, and how many times it is used. Import once, place it as often as the edit needs.

IMPORT FROM THE FINDER
Drag video, stills and audio straight onto the bin or the picture. One Import Media button takes all three and sorts them out for you.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
Playback, trimming, splitting, undo, export and every tool on a key. Press the keyboard icon in the editor for the ones available right now, or see the full list in Settings.

RIGHT-CLICK AND DRAG
Right-click a clip on the timeline for its options. Drag pictures and overlays on the canvas itself to move, scale and rotate them.

GRADING
Cinematic LUTs, curves, HSL, color wheels, masks and keyframes. Apple Log, Apple Log 2 and HLG HDR. ProRes 422 and ProRes 422 HQ export.
