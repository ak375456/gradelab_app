New audio tools, Relight, and editing improvements.

• Audio controls: Adjust volume, fades, stereo pan, and EQ, or start with a voice preset.
• Compressor: Tame loud moments with a simple Amount control and advanced settings.
• Auto Ducking: Choose a voice clip and automatically lower the music while the voice is active.
• Creative audio: Add echo, reverb, distortion, and bitcrusher. Try space presets and monitor each clip with a live level meter.
• Relight: Add virtual lights to videos you've already shot. Adjust their position, color, and intensity, or start with a preset.
• Color gradients make temperature, tint, hue, and saturation sliders easier to read.
• Emoji now render correctly alongside text in previews and exports.
• Recover trimmed footage with the left handle, even after moving a clip to the start of the timeline.
