Relight is here.

• Relight: add virtual lights to footage you've already shot. GradeLab estimates the shape of the scene, so light wraps around faces and subjects instead of sitting on top as a flat circle.
• Directional, point and spot lights, up to six per clip. Drag them on the picture with the pointer and keyframe every value.
• Presets: Soft Key, Warm Sunset, Cool Moonlight, Side Light, Top Light, Rim Light, Dramatic and Soft Fill.
• Works with SDR, HLG HDR and Apple Log, and with speed ramps, splits and reversed clips. Limit it to a mask to light one area only.
• Keyboard: ⇧⌘L opens Relight. The arrow keys nudge the selected light, with ⇧ for larger steps and ⌥ for finer ones. ⌫ deletes it and ⌘D duplicates it.
• Right-click a light for Duplicate, Disable, Reset and Delete.
• Interface improvements, bug fixes, and general app optimizations.
