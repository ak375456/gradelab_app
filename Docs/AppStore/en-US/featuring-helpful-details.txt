Two things set GradeLab apart.

It never guesses about colour. A format with no verified transform is refused with a reason, never quietly converted, and Apple Log 2 shipped only once its gamut was checked against the Academy Software Foundation's ACES config. Nobody's footage is rendered on an assumption.

And the real work is free: Apple Log and HDR grading, every tool live on your own footage, and 1080p exports with no watermark. Someone with an iPhone and no budget can finish a film in it.
