Apple Log 2 support, and a much faster look browser.

• Apple Log 2 — grade footage from iPhone 17 Pro. Apple Log 2 is decoded through its own Apple Wide Gamut transform rather than being treated as Apple Log, so the colour is right from the first frame, through grading and into the export.
• Looks load far faster — the look browser used to fill in slowly on large LUT collections, and applying a high-resolution LUT could take seconds. Look previews now appear almost immediately and a look applies as soon as you tap it.
• Fixed: the timecode above the timeline could wrap onto two lines on smaller iPhones and split a value across the break.
• Fixed: importing a photo said "Analyzing Video".
