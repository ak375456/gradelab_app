Speed ramping and optical flow are here.

• Speed: hold a constant rate, or build a curve. Place speed points anywhere on a clip and shape the ramp with eases and Bezier handles.
• Optical Flow: new frames are generated between the real ones, so slow motion stays sharp instead of stuttering. Frame Blending and Frame Sampling are there too.
• Freeze Frame and Reverse: hold a frame for as long as you want, or play a clip backwards.
• Presets: Montage, Hero, Bullet, Jump cut, Flash in and Flash out, each showing the curve it will make. Every one stays fully editable afterwards.
• Timeline: select two or more draggable rows and move them together.
• Optimized previews for smoother editing.
• Interface improvements, bug fixes, and general app optimizations.
