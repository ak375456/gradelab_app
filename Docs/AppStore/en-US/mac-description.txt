Turn everyday photos and videos into cinematic stories. GradeLab is a color grading editor for Mac with LUT filters, vintage looks, professional color tools and video editing, all in one app.

Pick a look, dial in its strength, then make it your own. From warm portraits and moody travel clips to soft film tones and retro aesthetics, GradeLab gives you full control over the light, color and atmosphere of every edit — across the whole frame or just one part of it.

BUILT FOR THE MAC
- A media bin beside the timeline lists every clip, photo and audio file, and how often each one is used.
- Drag media in from the Finder, then place it as many times as the edit needs.
- Keyboard shortcuts throughout, from playback and trimming to tools and export.
- Right-click a clip on the timeline to open its options.
- Move, scale and rotate pictures and overlays on the canvas itself.

CINEMATIC LUTS & VINTAGE FILTERS
- Browse cinematic LUTs with live previews on your own photos and videos.
- Signature looks like Warm Cinema, Teal Orange and Soft Film, plus a full film-inspired collection.
- Adjust look strength for a subtle finish or a bold cinematic style.
- Import your own .cube LUT files and use them just like built-in looks.
- Save your grading as presets and reuse it on any photo or video.

PROFESSIONAL COLOR GRADING
- Exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites and blacks.
- Temperature, tint, saturation and vibrance.
- Eight-band HSL to shape individual colors.
- Color wheels for shadows, midtones and highlights.
- Master, Red, Green and Blue curves.
- Hue, saturation and luminance curves for precise color control.
- Fade, sharpening and vignette.
- Before and after comparison as you edit.

MASKS & LOCAL GRADING
- Grade one part of the frame instead of the whole picture.
- Ellipse, rectangle, gradient and freehand masks.
- Feather the edge, set the strength, or invert a mask.
- Stack several masks on one clip, each with its own grade.
- Track a mask to a moving subject, forward or backward.

KEYFRAMED COLOR GRADING
- Animate the grade itself, so a look builds through a shot or falls away at the end.
- Keyframe exposure, contrast, temperature, saturation, HSL, color wheels, vignette, curves, film effects and look strength.

TRANSITIONS
- 45 transitions: dissolves, dips, wipes, slides, zooms, blur dissolve, whip pan, film burn, glitch, prism, page turn and more.

FILM EFFECTS
- Film grain, bloom, glow and halation for an authentic analog feel.

PROFESSIONAL SCOPES
- Histogram, waveform, RGB parade and vectorscope for accurate grading.

APPLE LOG, HDR & PRORES
- Grade Apple Log and Apple Log 2 footage, plus HLG HDR video.
- Apple Log 2 from iPhone 17 Pro uses its own wide-gamut transform, never Apple Log's.
- Export ProRes 422 and ProRes 422 HQ for a master that goes back into another edit.
- Export in 4K or your source resolution, with custom frame rate and bitrate.

EDIT VIDEO & PHOTOS
- Trim and split clips, change playback speed, and add titles and audio.
- Grade photos with the same color tools you use for video.
- Export photos as full-resolution JPEG, HEIC or lossless PNG.
- No watermarks on your exports.

TEXT, LAYERS & MOTION
- Add titles with curated font families or your own imported TTF fonts.
- Layer overlays with blend modes.
- Mask a layer with an ellipse, rectangle or split line.
- Feather or invert a layer mask, and keyframe it into a wipe or reveal.
- Animate text, transforms and color grading with keyframes.

Whether you're grading a travel vlog, a short film or a single portrait, GradeLab helps you create a look that's unmistakably yours.

Feature and format availability depend on your source media and your Mac. Some features require a subscription or one-time purchase.
