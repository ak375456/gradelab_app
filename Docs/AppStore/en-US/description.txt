Create cinematic photos and videos with GradeLab, a color grading editor for iPhone and iPad. Explore filters and LUTs, create your own presets, and bring a vintage film look or a clean, natural finish to your edits.

From warm portraits to moody travel clips, shape the light, color and atmosphere of every frame. Start with a look you love, adjust its strength, then make it your own.

CINEMATIC FILTERS & LUTs

Explore looks such as Warm Cinema, Teal Orange and Soft Film. Preview LUT filters on your own photos and videos, control their intensity, and fine-tune the result. Import your own .cube LUT files to bring your favorite color styles into GradeLab.

SAVE YOUR OWN PRESETS

Turn your grading adjustments into reusable presets. Apply a style you've created to another photo or video and build a consistent look across your work.

COLOR GRADING, YOUR WAY

Adjust exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites and blacks. Balance temperature and tint, and refine saturation and vibrance.

Shape individual colors with eight-band HSL. Use color wheels to balance shadows, midtones and highlights. Fine-tune tones with Master and RGB curves, or make targeted adjustments with hue, saturation and luminance curves.

Compare your edit with the original as you work. Check exposure and color with a histogram, waveform, RGB parade and vectorscope.

VINTAGE TEXTURE & FILM EFFECTS

Add film grain, soften highlights with bloom and glow, or create a film-inspired finish with halation. Refine your look with fade, sharpening and vignette, from subtle texture to a stronger cinematic style.

EDIT VIDEO & PHOTOS

Trim and split video clips, adjust playback speed, and add titles and audio. Build layered edits with overlays and blend modes. Animate supported text and transform settings with keyframes.

Use built-in font families or import TTF fonts to make your titles your own. Edit photos with the same color controls you use for video.

HDR, APPLE LOG & PRORES

Work with supported HLG HDR and Apple Log video. Export ProRes 422 or ProRes 422 HQ on compatible devices, with available formats determined by your footage and hardware.

FLEXIBLE EXPORTS

Export video in 4K or at supported source resolutions, with frame rate and bitrate controls. Save full-resolution photos as JPEG, HEIC or lossless PNG.

Feature and format availability depend on your source media and device capabilities.
