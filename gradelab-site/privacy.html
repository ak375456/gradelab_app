Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

GradeLab processes your editing media on your device. If you send in-app feedback, GradeLab sends your message and only the screenshot and device details you choose to include.

This policy covers GradeLab for iPhone, iPad and Mac and this support website. GradeLab is developed by Aftab Fazal Qayum.

Editing, camera and photo access

GradeLab processes your source videos, photos, recordings, and audio on your device. It stores projects, grades, presets, and imported LUTs and fonts locally. GradeLab uses Apple’s system picker to access items you select to edit or attach to feedback. It asks for permission to save exports to Photos and to use your camera or microphone when you choose those features.

When you export or share content, the destination you select handles the exported copy. If you use device backups, your device and backup settings determine what gets backed up.

Anonymous in-app feedback

Feedback goes directly to the GradeLab developer. GradeLab stores each report, including any screenshot, device details and developer reply, under a randomly generated report ID. GradeLab does not ask for a name, email address or account when you submit feedback.

You can open the feedback form from the home screen or from Settings → Feedback. When you submit a report, GradeLab sends the feedback type, app area, message, submission time and report ID to Google Cloud Firestore. You can attach one screenshot and can turn off the option to include app version and build, operating system version, and device model. The app shows the selected screenshot before you send it and lets you remove it.

I use this information to investigate problems, consider suggestions and reply. GradeLab keeps the private report ID and a copy of your sent message in your device’s Keychain. GradeLab allows one active report per device. It uses the ID to reopen your report and check for a reply. After GradeLab confirms the report was deleted, it removes the local feedback record and lets you send another report. Anyone with the private report ID can retrieve the report, so keep it private.

Your message or screenshot can identify you if it contains personal information. To stay anonymous, remove those details before sending. The app does not attach your projects, original recordings or device logs automatically.

Service providers and purchases

Google Cloud Firestore stores feedback and replies in the United States and processes them on my behalf under its service and data protection terms. GitHub Pages hosts this website and GitHub may process connection information, such as IP addresses, to operate it. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Apple handles purchases, subscription billing and refunds. GradeLab uses Apple’s StoreKit transaction information to verify and restore access to GradeLab Pro. I do not receive your card details or Apple Account credentials. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

GradeLab does not use advertising, in-app usage analytics, third-party crash reporting or cross-app tracking. I do not sell feedback or support correspondence or use it for advertising.

Support by email

If you email me, I receive your email address, message and any attachments you include. I use those details to answer you and investigate your request. I ask before quoting your feedback publicly.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Feedback, screenshots, replies and email correspondence are kept for as long as needed to investigate issues, respond and maintain a support record, subject to applicable legal obligations. Firestore feedback does not expire automatically. I may delete an attachment while keeping the written report and reply.

You can choose to skip feedback, remove the screenshot, or turn off device details before you submit. To request access, correction or deletion of feedback or support correspondence already sent, email ak375456@gmail.com. Include the approximate submission time and a short excerpt so I can locate your report. Please do not resend the screenshot. Open Settings → Feedback while online to let GradeLab confirm the deletion and clear the local copy.

Children

GradeLab is a general-purpose creative tool. It is not directed to children under 13.
